Spacing

2 captured

Fluid space scale Captured

13 steps plus 12 one-up pairs; refactored 2026-10-22 — no per-step clamp(): consumes the type scale’s shared --_progress and configuration, each step derived as round(multiplier × base, 0.0625rem) at both ends and interpolated by it (so --space-30’s constant 5px falls out of the rounding), and each pair interpolates from the lower step’s minimum to the upper step’s maximum with no numbers of its own; the original clamp() definitions kept as the floor where the shared-progress block is inactive or round() is unsupported; names and rendered values unchanged

Static spacing Captured
